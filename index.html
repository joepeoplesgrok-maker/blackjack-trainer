3. Why Card Counting Works (The Math)

Unlike roulette or slots, Blackjack has memory—cards already dealt are removed from the shoe until the next shuffle. Because rules treat players and dealers asymmetrically, a shoe rich in 10s and Aces shifts expected value in your favor:

  • 3:2 Blackjack Bonus: Both you and the dealer get more Blackjacks in high counts, but yours pays 1.5x while the dealer's only takes 1.0x. (Never play 6:5 tables—they add ~1.39% to house edge.)
  • Dealer Must Hit Stiffs (12–16): You can stand on 12–16; the dealer is forced to hit and busts far more often when the shoe is dense with 10s.
  • Doubling & Splitting Pay More: Doubling on 10 or 11 lands a 20 or 21 much more often in positive counts.
  • Deck Count & Penetration: Fewer decks reduce base house edge (2D S17/DAS ≈ -0.19% vs 6D ≈ -0.41% vs 8D ≈ -0.43%), while deeper cut-card penetration creates extreme True Counts much more often.
4. Running Count, Cancellation & True Count

Hi-Lo Tag Values

  • 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 → +1 (Low cards removed = stronger remaining shoe)
  • 7, 8, 9 → 0 (Neutral—ignore them completely)
  • 10, J, Q, K, A → -1 (High cards removed = weaker remaining shoe)

Pro Speed Technique: Pair Cancellation

Don't count cards one by one as they hit the felt. Wait until each player has two cards, then cancel pairs: a King (-1) and a 4 (+1) sum to 0—ignore both! Practice this in Speed Count Drill → 2 Cards (Pairs).

Converting to True Count

A Running Count of +6 with 6 decks left is diluted (+1.0 per deck). With 1.5 decks left in Double Deck or late in a 6D shoe, it's a monster (+4.0 per deck). Always divide by unseen remaining decks (including the dealer's hole card and burn card):

True Count (TC) = Running Count ÷ Decks Remaining

Every +1 TC shifts your advantage by roughly +0.50%.

5. Betting Strategies, Kelly Criterion & Wonging

Card counting generates ~80% of its profit from bet sizing and ~20% from playing deviations.

Bet Spreads by Game Type

  • Double Deck (2D): A 1–6 or 1–8 unit spread beats the ~0.19%–0.39% house edge cleanly without extreme casino heat.
  • 6-Deck & 8-Deck Shoes: Typically require a 1–12 or 1–16 unit spread to overcome the ~0.41%–0.65% base house edge.

The Kelly Criterion (Dynamic Bankroll Sizing)

Full Kelly Bet % = Edge ÷ Variance (σ² ≈ 1.33)
Half-Kelly Bet = Bankroll × 0.5 × (Edge ÷ 1.33)

Half-Kelly (selectable in ⚙ Rules → Betting Strategy) achieves 75% of the maximum theoretical growth rate while dramatically cutting your Risk of Ruin (RoR).

Wonging (Back-Counting)

Wonging In means sitting down only at TC ≥ +1/+2. Wonging Out means leaving whenever TC hits -2 or worse. Watch the HUD's WONG indicator in Table Play!

6. Advanced Concepts & Other Counting Systems

Spreading to Two Hands

At high True Counts (TC ≥ +2 or +3), playing two spots at ~75% of your single-hand Kelly bet each lowers covariance-adjusted variance while pushing more total EV onto the table.

Casino Heat & Cover Play

  • Avoid robotic 1-to-12 jumps right after a losing hand; ramp naturally after wins when possible.
  • Splitting 10s (I18 #4 & #5) is the fastest way to draw pit attention—many pros skip it for longevity.
  • Keep sessions under 45–60 minutes per shift.

Other Counting Systems

  • KO (Knock-Out): Unbalanced count (7s are +1) that eliminates True Count division entirely.
  • Zen Count & Omega II: Level-2 balanced systems (+2/+1/0/-1/-2) gaining ~5–10% higher playing efficiency than Hi-Lo.
  • Hi-Opt II + Ace Side Count: Elite accuracy for single/double-deck pitch games.
